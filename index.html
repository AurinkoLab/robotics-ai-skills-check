AI & Robotics course · ages 12+

Skills Check

Before the course starts, have a go at a few robotics, coding and AI puzzles. Try them at home, at your own pace.

This is not an exam, and you cannot fail it. It's completely fine if some things don't work out. We just want to see what you already know and what you enjoy, so that the course is interesting for you: not too easy, and not too hard.

What to do

1
Get ready
Paper and a pencil for rough work, a calculator if you like, and about 25 minutes somewhere quiet.
2
Try 13 puzzles in 3 parts
They get harder as you go. Most puzzles have an “I don't know” option, and choosing it is a good answer. Then answer a few quick questions about yourself.
3
Send us your answers
At the end you can copy, download or email them to us. After that you'll see the right answers and explanations.

How far should I get?

Part 1 is a warm-up. Part 2 needs a bit of logic and some simple maths. Part 3 is meant to be hard, and most people won't solve all of it. That's expected.

If you get stuck, skip the puzzle and move on. The questions about you at the end matter just as much as the puzzles.

Why we ask

Not to give you a score. We'd like to know:

  • what you already know,
  • what you enjoy most,
  • whether the course will be interesting for you, or whether you'd like something more challenging.
