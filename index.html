Skills Check

Before the course starts, have a go at some robotics, coding and AI puzzles at four different levels. Try them at home, at your own pace.

This is not an exam, and you cannot fail it. It's completely fine if some things don't work out. We just want to see what you already know and what you enjoy, so that the course is interesting for you: not too easy, and not too hard.

Four levels

Each level has one puzzle on each topic: 💻 code, ⚙️ robots, 🧩 logic and 🧠 AI.

What to do

1
Get ready
Paper and a pencil for rough work, a calculator if you like, and about 30 minutes somewhere quiet.
2
Go as far as you can
Most puzzles have an “I don't know” option, and choosing it is a good answer. If a level feels too hard, you can skip straight to the questions about you.
3
Tell us about yourself
A few quick questions about what you've done and what you enjoy. They matter just as much as the puzzles.
4
Send us your answers
Copy, download or email them to us. After that you'll see the right answers and explanations.

Why we ask

Not to give you a score. We'd like to know:

  • what you already know,
  • what you enjoy most,
  • whether the course will be interesting for you, or whether you'd like something more challenging.

Your answers are saved in this browser as you go, so you can close the page and come back later.
